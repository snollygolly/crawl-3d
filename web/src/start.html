Crawl 3D

Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup 0.34, running entirely in your browser, with a 3D view.

  • Arrow keys move relative to the camera. Shift+arrow runs.
  • Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click a square to travel, right-click to describe.
  • All the normal Crawl keys work (vi-keys and numpad move in fixed directions).
  • Your game saves in this browser. Ctrl-S saves and quits.
